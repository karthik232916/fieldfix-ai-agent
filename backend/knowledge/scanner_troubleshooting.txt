Warehouse Scanner Troubleshooting Guide

Device: Warehouse Scanner

Problem: Scanner does not turn on

Possible Causes:
1. Battery is completely discharged.
2. Power cable is disconnected or damaged.
3. Charging adapter is not supplying power.
4. Power button is stuck or not responding.
5. Device firmware may require an update.
6. Internal hardware damage may have occurred.

Troubleshooting Steps:
1. Connect the scanner to its approved power adapter.
2. Check whether the charging indicator appears.
3. Leave the scanner charging for at least 15 minutes.
4. Try turning the scanner on again.
5. If the scanner still does not turn on, try a hard reset if supported by the device.
6. Inspect the power cable and connector for visible damage.
7. If there is physical damage, contact maintenance or vendor support.

Safety:
Do not open the scanner or perform internal electrical repairs unless authorized and qualified to do so.
